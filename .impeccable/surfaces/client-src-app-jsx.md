# Landing page (client/src/App.jsx)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: leisure readers and university students in Bangladesh, weighted equally. Action: search by title, author or ISBN, or open a genre and view a book; create an account to order. Proof: real seed catalogue facts (ISBN, year, price, live stock), the order route, and the receipt promises. Constraints: no invented testimonials or claims; keep the simulated-payments and CSE327 disclosure; mid-range Android phones.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The catalogue is a walk along book-fair stalls: each genre is a stall with its cloth banner, and the counter shows the real editions. It refuses the default bookstore arrangement of a search hero, a bestseller carousel and a genre card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Cloth-banner colour fields at page scale: banner red #C8332B, marigold #F2B233, canopy green #1F5A3A, white enamel signboards and warm ink #1A1412, with genre banners in indigo, rani pink, turquoise and jute brown (Academic, so it reads against the canopy green). Triangular bunting strings, bamboo-pole frames, painted signboard lettering in Anek Bangla (condensed, heavy) with Bangla labels beside the English, and Hind Siliguri for text. No cards, no gradients and no icon tiles.

STORY: The visitor recognises the fair, learns that this store shows the exact edition and live stock, browses a stall, and searches or creates an account.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Bunting across the top edge. A white signboard nav strip holds the wordmark, links and auth. A drenched red banner field carries a giant white headline (left, roughly 7 columns). The information-desk signboard on two bamboo poles (right) holds the working search plus quick-ask links. The stall fronts begin at the bottom edge.

FORM: Boi Mela Stalls, position 1 on the ordered list (pick card, chosen by the user); seed:d0e8c0a17b08e654. Signature interaction: choosing a stall lowers its banner and lays its books on the counter in a fixed label grid (title, author, ISBN, year, price, stock). Motion grammar: the bunting sways, and the banner drops with a staggered lay-down.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
